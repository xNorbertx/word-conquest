Changes made by Word Conquest: select listed lowercase full word forms; exclude
SALDO proper names, abbreviations, multiword entries, affixes and compound-only
fragments; normalize Unicode to NFC; remove decomposable accents except those
on the Swedish letters å/ä/ö; retain only 3–69 A–Z/Å/Ä/Ö letters; merge with the
original list, deduplicate and sort. Examples: é/è/ê/ë → e, ü → u, ñ → n, ç → c.
Punctuation, spaces and foreign letters without a decomposable board spelling
are not removed or guessed. No new compounds or inflections are invented.

The resulting list contains 991,634 unique playable forms. New games pin its
version; previous games retain their original dictionary. Letter distribution
and scoring are unchanged. Uncommon or offensive words may be present.

This release does not include SAOL data and is not the Wordfeud dictionary.
