Language-specific edge placement profiles, version 1 (28 October 2026).
edge-profiles-v1.json is an adaptation of wordfreq 3.1.1 frequency data,
intersected with Word Conquest's pinned English, Swedish and Dutch dictionaries.
Profile data and published research measurements: CC BY-SA 4.0.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
Changes: 3-7-letter legal forms, frequency threshold Zipf 3.5, accent normalization,
per-word letter/pair counts and normalized undirected pair strengths. Swedish
retains A-Z/Å/Ä/Ö; Dutch uses I + J. The accepted game dictionaries are unchanged.
