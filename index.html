How to play

Drag across letters, or tap them one at a time. Tap the previous tile to undo a step. You may reuse words on later turns if you can find them again.

Jokers (?) can be any A–Z letter. Trace your path, then fill in the joker letters below your word. Each joker can be a different letter every turn, even after capture.

Castles keep their value when captured. Most territory points after the final turn wins.
