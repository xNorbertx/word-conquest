    How to play

    Start on a tile you own and connect at least three letters, including diagonals. Use each tile once. Claim neutral territory and at most three enemy tiles. If all your territory is gone, start anywhere. Choose one A–Z letter for each joker.

    Your score is permanent word points plus current territory: 1 per ordinary tile, 3 per castle. Longer words earn a bonus. Used ordinary letters change after each turn; jokers stay wild. You can refresh your owned letters instead of playing a word, earning no word points.

    Share a 120-letter budget. If Player 1 uses the last letters, Player 2 gets one final reply. Full words remain allowed. Highest final score wins; equal scores draw. Repeated words are allowed.

    English words are checked against a fixed Letterpress-derived list. Listed inflections and slang count; unlisted words, punctuation and abbreviations not in the list do not. It may contain archaic or offensive words and is not a family-filtered dictionary. No live dictionary changes affect an existing game.

    Privacy & support

    Only your opponents can see shared games and display names. Your email is used for sign-in, recovery and optional reminders. We use Supabase for storage and identity, Resend for sending email, and the operator’s Zoho inbox for support. No ads, public profile search or analytics trackers. You can export your data or delete your account in Account. Shared history is then anonymized; backups expire under the host’s retention policy.
